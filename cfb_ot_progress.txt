Overview
--------
A Streamlit app for pricing and tracking College Football overtime markets.
Given two FBS teams, user-defined strength parameters, and a live score, the
app computes drive outcome probabilities, current OT period win/tie/advance
probabilities, and a full game-length market table covering OT 1 through OT 7+.

The app correctly models the two CFB overtime rule regimes:
  - OT 1–2: Full possession OT (each team gets one possession from the 25-yd line).
    Drive outcomes: TD+PAT (7), TD+2pt (8), TD (6 / missed PAT), FG (3),
    Turnover / Missed FG (0), Defensive TD (6 for the defense).
  - OT 3+: 2-pt conversion shootout (each team runs one play from the 3-yd line).
    Outcome per team: Success (2pts) or Failure (0pts).

State is fully managed via st.session_state so the OT history, scores, and
period counter persist through user interactions.

v0.1 Changelog
--------------
- Built complete Streamlit app from scratch, self-contained, no external data files.
- Implemented all 134 FBS teams in a sorted dropdown for Away/Home selection.
- Sidebar controls:
    * Team selection dropdowns (Away / Home).
    * "Overall Strength" slider (-1.0 to +1.0, step 0.05) — shifts TD vs FG/turnover
      rates for the offensive team. Dynamic label shows favored team or "Even".
    * "Off/Def Tendency" slider (-1.0 to +1.0, step 0.05) — scales the overall
      scoring environment up or down. Dynamic label shows game type.
    * Away / Home score inputs (synced with session_state).
    * Compact OT history scoreboard table (standard OT shows TD-7/FG-3/--- labels;
      shootout shows checkmark/X per team).
    * "Possession order" radio — Away first vs Home first per period.
    * Current situation panel:
        - Standard OT: field position slider (1–99), down selectbox, distance input,
          plus per-team outcome selectboxes for logging.
        - Shootout (OT 3+): simplified Success/Failure radio per team.
    * "Log Result" button — records period outcome, updates scores, advances period.
    * "Reset" button — clears all history and resets to OT 1, score 0-0.
- Probability engine (base_drive_probs):
    * Starts from calibrated base rates: td_pat=35%, td_2pt=5%, td_6=3%,
      fg=20%, turnover=32%, def_td=5%.
    * Applies off_def_tendency multipliers to scoring categories then renormalizes.
    * Applies strength_delta multipliers for the specific offense then renormalizes.
    * Away and home each get their own probs (strength_delta sign flips for home).
- period_outcome_probs: full enumeration of all 36 away×home outcome combinations,
  yielding P(away wins period), P(home wins period), P(advances to next OT).
- shootout_period_probs: closed-form P(away wins), P(home wins), P(tied) for the
  2-pt conversion shootout, with success probability scaled by off_def_tendency.
- game_length_table: chains period-level probabilities to produce cumulative
  P(ends this OT) and P(game still live) for OT 1 through OT 7+.
- Main panel sections:
    1. Drive Outcome Probabilities — Altair donut charts for Away and Home drives;
       expandable breakdown table.
    2. Current OT Period Outcome — Altair donut chart + metric cards for
       away wins / home wins / advances.
    3. Game Length Markets — styled DataFrame table + Altair bar chart showing
       the game-length distribution across OT 1–7+.
- No scipy, no external data files; only stdlib + streamlit + altair + pandas.
- Run with: streamlit run cfb_ot_app.py

Fixed — 2026-07-12
------------------
- Moneyline win-prob bug: game_win_probs was reusing the current period's
  live/collapsed drive distributions for all hypothetical FUTURE periods, so
  a team that had just scored was assumed to score again with certainty every
  future OT. This massively over-favored the first team to score (post-TD home
  win prob showed ~19% when it should be ~29%). Fixed by threading separate
  future_away_probs/future_home_probs (the neutral base dists) into the
  recursion. Committed 056ee12. (Even-matched post-TD home win prob now ~29%.)

- Two-step TD scoring: pressing TD no longer scores immediately. It enters a
  pending state (pending_td) that (a) converges the ball team's drive
  distribution onto the three TD point totals 6/7/8, renormalized, in both the
  drive pie and the moneyline, and (b) swaps the TD/FG/TO button row for a
  +2 / +1 / +0 row. Those finalize as td_2pt (8), td_pat (7), td_6 (6)
  respectively via the existing _log_result path, so scoring stays correct for
  both first- and second-possession cases. Verified win probs: Away-first TD
  then +1 -> home 29%, +2 -> home 2%, +0 -> home 55%. Full Reset clears
  pending_td cleanly. Shootout (OT3+) flow unchanged.
